Suicides Hungary 2024

There are 4 suicides per day in Hungary

In 2024 occurred 1,601 suicides in Hungary. This year, 8 more people committed suicide than in 2023. In 2024, on average 4 Hungarians committed suicide every day.

Hungary had 16.78 suicides per 100,000 inhabitants, well above the world average of 9.2 suicides per 100,000 people.

Hungary is a country with a high rate of suicide.

Of the 1,601 recorded suicide cases in 2024, 1,248 were men suicides and 353 were women. So that, the suicide rate among males were 27.1 per 100,000 men and for women 7.15 per 100,000 female.

Statistics show that in 2014, suicides were the main cause of unnatural death in the world. In Hungary suicide also is one of the leading causes of unnatural death. By comparison, there were 70 homicides in Hungary, the number of suicides far outnumber homicides. For every homicide there were 22.9 suicides.
